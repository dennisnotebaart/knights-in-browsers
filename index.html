    How to Play

    The idea. Build a working medieval economy, then turn it into an army. Everything is carried by serfs along roads, built by labourers, and every house needs a worker trained at the School for one gold each.

    Building. Open the Build tab, click a house and place it. Its door must face a road: draw roads from the yellow entrance tile back to the storehouse. Serfs bring timber and stone to the site and a labourer builds it. Fields for farms and wine fields for vineyards are placed like roads.

    Food. Citizens get hungry. An Inn stocked with bread, sausages or wine keeps them working. Starving units die. Soldiers are fed by serfs automatically as long as there is food in a storehouse.

    Chains. Woodcutter → Sawmill → timber. Quarry → stone. Farm → Mill → Bakery → bread. Farm → Swine farm → Butcher → sausages, and skins → Tannery → leather. Mines need deposits: coal + iron ore → Iron smithy → steel; coal + gold ore → Metallurgist → gold.

    Army. Train recruits at the School; they walk to the Barracks. Weapons and armour are delivered there. Click a soldier type in the Barracks panel to equip a recruit. Select soldiers by clicking or dragging a box, right-click to move, right-click an enemy to attack. Halt, Split, Link and column buttons control formations. Pikes beat cavalry, cavalry beats archers, archers beat everything from a distance.

    Controls. Arrow keys / WASD / mouse at screen edge scroll. Mouse wheel zooms. Right-click cancels placement. Esc clears the selection. Space pauses. Keys 1-3 set game speed. Click a message to jump to it. Save any time from the Menu tab.
